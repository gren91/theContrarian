The Case Against
Raising Rates

Inflation concerns are overstated due to measurement distortions. Core CPI sits at 2.3% (near target), while PCE elevation is driven by just two items: portfolio management fees and flash memory prices—neither relevant to average consumers.

Key Arguments:
  • Inflation data shows no wage-price spiral
  • Corporate credit spreads signal no distress
  • Rate hikes won't slow AI infrastructure spend
  • Consumer expectations remain anchored

Based on analysis from Fundstrat Global Advisors, Federal Reserve data, and market indicators.
